NDIS Wiki — Proposed Information Architecture
Working sitemap v0.1  |  19 August 2026
A participant-first alternative to the current NDIS website structure.
The design begins with the two questions a person is most likely to know the answer to: “I am on the NDIS” or “I want to apply for the NDIS”. A persistent Resources area sits outside both pathways and remains accessible from every page.

Recommended structure for every wiki article:
"Every substantive page should answer the practical question first, then expose its evidence.
• Plain-English answer
• What you can do now
• What evidence or documents you may need
• Deadlines / time limits, where applicable
• What can go wrong / common administrative problems
• If you disagree: review, complaint or escalation route
• Legal basis — Act / Rules / instrument
• NDIA guidance — clearly labelled as guidance, not law
• Change history — previous rule, current rule, commencement
• Sources — official live link + archived copy + retrieval/archive date"
