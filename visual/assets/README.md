## 📐 Usage Guidelines & Clear Space

- **Clear Space**: Maintain a minimum margin around the logo mark equal to 25% of the mark's height.
- **Minimum Size**: Do not render the mark smaller than `20px x 20px` in digital interfaces.
- **Do Not**: Distort stroke ratios, alter the core brand colors (`#172A52` Navy or `#FFFFFF` White), or place on low-contrast backgrounds.
