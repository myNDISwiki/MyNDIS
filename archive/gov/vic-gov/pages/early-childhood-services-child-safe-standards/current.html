Child Safe Standards guidance for early childhood services

Best practice advice for implementing the Child Safe Standards in early childhood services.

All early childhood services in Victoria must comply with the Child Safe Standards.

The Victorian Early Childhood Regulatory Authority (VECRA) is the integrated sector regulator of the Standards in the early childhood sector.

Read about How we regulate the Child Safe Standards.
