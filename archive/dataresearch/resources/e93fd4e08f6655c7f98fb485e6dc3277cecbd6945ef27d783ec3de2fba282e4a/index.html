Detailed dashboards for service districts in Tasmania, Australian Capital Territory and other territories

The detailed dashboard provides detailed market monitoring information on a specific district. A dashboard for each of the 4 districts in Tasmania, the ACT district and other territories – all covered in the December 2021 analysis is available below.

Please note that the dashboards below are not accessible. The accessible versions are available in CSV format in the Accessible data section below.

Files larger than 20MB are zipped or compressed. To unzip files to your computer:

  1. Double click the link with the files you wish to access.
  2. Select an option to unzip files to your preferred folder location.
  3. Access the documents on your computer.
