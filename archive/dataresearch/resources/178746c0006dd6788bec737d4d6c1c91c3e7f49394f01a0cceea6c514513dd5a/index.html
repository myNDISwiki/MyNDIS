Market monitoring

  • Data notes 
  • The NDIS market as at 31 December 2021
  • Specialist disability accommodation (SDA) quarterly report
  • Provider sentiment and satisfaction survey

The Market Dashboards are part of a series of publicly shared datasets released by the NDIA as part of the Agency’s commitment to sharing more specialist in-depth data.

The dashboards explore the performance and opportunities across the market by comparing key market indicators by geographical regions and participant characteristics.

The latest market data is now available in the interactive explore data tool.

Data notes 

  • All data is de-identified and shared in CSV format, with data guidance in Microsoft Word documents.
  • This release uses ABS classified Local Government Areas (LGA) 2011.

There are separate dashboards for ‘participants receiving SIL/SDA’, ‘participants not receiving SIL/SDA’ and a combined dashboard for ‘all participants’.

Regional dashboards (service district and LGA)

The regional dashboards provide detailed information on a specific service district or LGA. Dashboards for each of the 80 service districts and dashboards for 505 out of the 575 LGAs in Australia are available. 

Note that we do not provide dashboards for 70 LGAs where the NDIA participant population of that LGA consists of 10 or fewer participants.  

Please use the state and territory links below to access the links to the regional dashboards for that state or territory.
