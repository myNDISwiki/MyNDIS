Participant, families and carer outcomes reports

Listen
Save page as PDF

On this page:

  • Participant outcomes reports to 30 June 2024 
  • Families and carer outcomes reports to 30 June 2024
  • National outcomes dashboard to 30 June 2024 
  • Regional outcomes dashboards by service district and LGA to 30 June 2024 

The outcomes framework surveys measure the outcomes of participants and their families and carers who are part of the NDIS. 

The surveys ask participants about how their lives are changing in areas like daily living, choice and control, health and wellbeing, relationships, community participation, work and learning. Families and carers are also asked about their experiences while caring for a person in the scheme. 

The responses of participants and their families and carers are helping to understand how the Scheme is making a difference while pointing to areas where improvements may be needed. 

The results of the outcomes surveys include analysis shared through the latest outcomes reports, dashboards and data downloads for service districts and to LGA level.

National outcomes dashboard to 30 June 2024 

The information gathered on outcomes is also available at a national level. The national dashboard summarises the outcomes for NDIS participants, their families and carers from responses received through the outcomes framework surveys.

Regional outcomes dashboards by service district and LGA to 30 June 2024 

The regional dashboards provide more detail on NDIS participant and family and carer outcomes for a service district or LGA.  

Dashboards for each of the 80 service districts and dashboards for 539 out of the 566 LGAs in Australia are available. Noting that dashboards are not provided for LGAs with less than 20 NDIS participants.
