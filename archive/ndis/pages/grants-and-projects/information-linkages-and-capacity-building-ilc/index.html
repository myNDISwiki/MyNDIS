From mid-2020, the ILC Program transitioned from the NDIA to the Department of Social Services (DSS). 

In May 2025, responsibilities for disability were moved to the Department of Health, Disability and Ageing (DHDA). For more information about the ILC Program, please visit the DHDA ILC webpage .
