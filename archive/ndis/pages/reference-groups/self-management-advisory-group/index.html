Self-Management Advisory Group

Listen
Save page as PDF

The NDIA established the Self-Management Advisory Group in 2024. 

The Group provides advice to the NDIA on changes to the NDIS that impact people who self-manage their plans. This includes advice on implementing NDIS Reform and the Self-management policy.  

The Self-Management Advisory Group will help shape how the NDIA co-designs and engage with the disability community about changes that impact self-management. 

Membership 

Membership includes self-managed participants, child representatives and nominees, as well as representatives from the disability sector and the Independent Advisory Council. 

Members are: 

  • Sam Paior – Independent Advisory Council member (Co-Chair)
  • Marita Walker – Subject matter expert
  • Marguerite Visser – Australian Inclusion Group
  • Jane Wardlaw – Independent Advisory Council member
  • Ken Griffits – Participant Representative
  • Sylvana Mahmic – Participant Representative
  • Dariane McLean – Inclusion Australia
  • Jan Kruger – Imagine More
  • Suzanne Gearing – Physical Disability Australia (PDA)
