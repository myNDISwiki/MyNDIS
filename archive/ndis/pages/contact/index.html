National Contact Centre open Monday to Friday 8am to 8pm (local time).

First Nations Connect is a phone service for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples and their families, representatives, health workers, and community organisations. 

Chat online


Our live webchat can help with any general enquiries. For urgent or complex matters, please call or email us. We are available from Monday to Friday, 8am to 8pm.
