Historical Agreements

Intergovernmental Agreement

On 7 December 2012, COAG signed an Intergovernmental Agreement (IGA) for the NDIS Launch.

States and territories signed bilateral agreements with the Commonwealth detailing NDIS operational and funding arrangements for each trial site.

These agreements included the planned intake of participants and the balance of cash and in-kind contributions to the Scheme.

Heads of Agreement

The Commonwealth reached agreements to establish the full NDIS with all states and territories. 

The Heads of Agreement for each state and territory outlined the transition to full scheme arrangements and timeframes (see links below).
