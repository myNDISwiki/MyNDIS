Media enquiries

For all media enquiries, please email [email protected] or call the media duty officer on 0477 703 960.

For all other enquiries please phone 1800 800 110.

Note: The Media team cannot answer questions about a participant’s supports or escalate individual matters. Anyone who is not from a media outlet should continue to contact the enquiries line on 1800 800 110.
